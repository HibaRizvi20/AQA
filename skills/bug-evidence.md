# Bug evidence
Evidence must **demonstrate the defect**, not just show the screen. Capture the exact reproduction:
the request/args, the actual response/state, and a screenshot pinned to the moment the defect is
visible. If it does not reproduce deterministically, it is not yet a bug - keep investigating.
